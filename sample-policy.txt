GLOBAL ENTERPRISE EMPLOYEE TRAVEL & EXPENSE POLICY

Document ID: POL-2026-TRV-004
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 3.2 (Supercedes Version 3.1)
Classification: Internal Enterprise Standard
Applies To: All full-time, part-time, and contract employees globally


1. OVERVIEW & POLICY STATEMENT

This policy defines the governance, approval workflows, and reimbursement limits for business travel undertaken on behalf of the company. Employees are expected to exercise prudent judgment when incurring business expenses.


2. SPENDING THRESHOLDS & ALLOWANCE LIMITS

2.1 Domestic Travel Expense Allowances
For domestic travel within the country of primary employment, the maximum allowable reimbursements without executive escalation are detailed below.

STANDARD DOMESTIC ALLOWANCE LIMITS:

- Lodging (Standard): $250 per night. Receipt required (itemized). Excludes minibar, room service, and personal entertainment.
- Meals & Incidentals: $75 per day (Per Diem). Receipt not required if under $25. Itemized receipts required for single meals exceeding $25.
- Ground Transportation: $100 per day. Receipt required. Covers rideshare (Uber/Lyft), taxi, or public transit.
- General Travel Expenses: $500 total per trip. Receipt required. Aggregated limit for miscellaneous approved travel costs.

Rule TRV-201: Any single expense exceeding $500 requires pre-approval from a Senior Director or Vice President prior to booking.


3. RECEIPT & DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

3.1 Standard Documentation
1. Itemization: Receipts must show individual line items, date, vendor name, and payment method. Summary credit card charge slips alone are not acceptable.
2. Exceptions: Receipts are waived for incidental expenses (e.g., parking meters, highway tolls) strictly under $25.00 USD.

3.2 Missing Receipts
If a receipt is lost or unobtainable, the employee must submit a signed Missing Receipt Affidavit (Form FIN-102). Frequent reliance on affidavits (more than 3 per calendar year) will trigger a mandatory internal audit.


4. APPROVAL WORKFLOWS & PRE-AUTHORIZATION

4.1 Domestic Travel
Direct Manager Approval is required via the internal portal at least 14 business days prior to departure.

4.2 International Travel
Pre-Approval Requirement: All international travel requires dual pre-approval from both the employee's Direct Manager and the VP of Global Operations prior to booking any flights or accommodation.
Passport & Visa Processing: Expedited visa or passport processing fees are reimbursable only if required for emergency business travel requested within 5 business days of departure.


5. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES

The following items are strictly non-reimbursable under any circumstances:
- Alcohol and tobacco products (unless part of a pre-approved client entertainment dinner).
- Flight upgrades (e.g., Economy to Business or First Class), unless the flight duration exceeds 10 consecutive hours.
- Airline lounge membership fees or day passes.
- Traffic or parking violations incurred using personal or rental vehicles.
- Personal grooming, laundry services (for trips under 5 days), or minibar items.


6. POLICY EXCEPTIONS & ESCALATIONS

Exceptions to this policy require written authorization from the Chief Financial Officer (CFO). Requests must be submitted using Form POL-EXC-01 at least 5 business days prior to incurring the expense.
